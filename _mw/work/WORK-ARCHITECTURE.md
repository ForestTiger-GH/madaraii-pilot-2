## Purpose and boundary

Organize one durable development contour for a CBR unified-statistics software product. The contour begins from a preserved human carrier, investigates the registry-defined Bank of Russia source universe, forms maintained knowledge and accepted target semantics, realizes and verifies the product, integrates it, and closes with cold-recoverable state.

The project boundary is exactly the one declared in `WORKSPACE.md`. Other repositories and prior project knowledge are excluded except MADARAII and the single permitted `stratbox/.../registry.py` input file.

- **Work State:** `WORK-CBR-001` — exact contour status, Baselines, established Results, residue, next safe action.
- **Raw input:** `INBOX-CBR-001` — immutable provenance carrier, never current Product/Knowledge/Decision truth.
- **Developed input Result:** `RES-ACTOR-001` — normalized semantic acts and routes.

## Work geometry

1. **Bootstrap and intake.** Preserve the exact source carrier; develop its semantic acts; establish current Work State and reliable routes.
2. **Source-universe Research.** Resolve the exact registry source list, acquire every source, census files/sheets/tables, identify structural families and outliers, then research materially distinct semantic problems. Acquisition/probing may use temporary automation but its outputs are Research evidence, not Product truth.
3. **Knowledge fan-in.** Reconcile source studies and external evidence into maintained Scientific Knowledge with explicit UNKNOWNs, collision rules, provenance requirements, and bounded completeness.
4. **Target semantics.** Form Target WHAT from authorized intent plus admitted knowledge; then Target HOW. Separate architecture follows only if it protects independent realization responsibilities.
5. **Realization.** Form only the implementation planning depth required by the accepted target; implement reusable acquisition, inspection/parsing, normalization/catalog, storage/export, bilingual semantics, querying/pivoting, validation, and notebook-facing surfaces as justified by the target.
6. **Assurance and integration.** Verify exact claims on real source files, including preservation/reconciliation tests; integrate only verified state; reconcile documentation/current knowledge where actual implementation changes factual understanding.
7. **Closure.** Audit Product, Knowledge, evidence, Work State, open Questions/Findings and restartability. Close the contour when the commissioned need is materially satisfied rather than extending scope for aesthetic completeness.

Dependencies follow these semantic prerequisites. Independent source-family studies may fan out after a complete source census; target formation waits for adequate knowledge fan-in; Product admission waits for verification. Integration serializes the current Product transition.

## Authority boundaries

The user Commission authorizes the complete contour and repository mutation inside this project boundary. Research results and agent design outputs remain candidates until their owner transition is recorded. Source evidence cannot itself establish a Product Decision. Verification produces reliance evidence and does not silently repair candidates. Product admission occurs only after the verification claims required by Target WHAT are satisfied or explicitly bounded.

## Continuity and recovery

Every material checkpoint updates `WORK-CBR-001` with exact governing revision, source-universe Baseline, established owner locators/revisions, completed evidence, unresolved material residue, and one next safe action. A new session rehydrates from `WORKSPACE.md` and `WORK-CBR-001`, then re-resolves owners and sources. Chat/model memory is discovery-only.

Stable identities live in artifact payloads/front-door mappings; filenames are locators. Generated artifacts identify their authoritative inputs and are rebuildable. No source spreadsheet is committed solely for convenience unless retention becomes materially justified and repository limits permit it.
